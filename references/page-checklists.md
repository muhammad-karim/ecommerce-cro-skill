# Page-by-page CRO checklist

Work through these on **mobile first**, then desktop. Mark each item ✅ pass / ⚠️ weak / ❌ missing, and note the evidence. Not every item applies to every store. Skip those that don't fit the product or price point, and note why.

**⚖️** = mixed evidence. Recommend testing this rather than shipping it blind.

## 1. Global
- [ ] Announcement bar contrasts with the site, is visible on mobile, and links only an underlined CTA phrase
- [ ] Trust policy bar under the nav (shipping, returns, guarantee) — not buried in the footer
- [ ] Body text ≥16px, line height ~1.5, line length ~60–80 characters, dark-on-light contrast
- [ ] Every element earns its place. Remove clutter and competing CTAs
- [ ] Contact options visible (phone, chat, email). Being contactable builds trust
- [ ] No fake urgency, fake reviews or fake "as seen in" logos (a legal risk as well as a trust risk)

## 2. Homepage
- [ ] Within ~5 seconds it's clear what the store sells and why to buy here (a benefit-led headline describing exactly what they do)
- [ ] Social proof and trust visible immediately (rating + count, a customer quote, press logos if real)
- [ ] **No auto-rotating hero slider.** A large hero shouldn't push products down; get visitors to best sellers fast
- [ ] One clear primary path, e.g. "Shop best sellers". The main audiences or categories are visible above the fold
- [ ] Search is prominent, ideally with a suggested search term
- [ ] Key selling points just below the fold

## 3. Navigation and mega menu
- [ ] Top-level nav goes straight to products and categories. Shopping links aren't buried in deep dropdowns
- [ ] Mega menu has category images and thumb-sized tap targets on mobile
- [ ] A "Trending now" or bestsellers row with real products and prices at the top of the menu
- [ ] A one-line description under each category, where categories genuinely differ
- [ ] Collections ordered by revenue per session, not internal preference
- [ ] No social icons or off-site links in the menu
- [ ] Revenue links separated from generic links (about, blog)
- [ ] Free-shipping message and a line of social proof in the menu

## 4. Collection / category page
- [ ] **No big hero banner** pushing products down
- [ ] 2×2 products visible above the fold on mobile
- [ ] **Prices shown** on product cards
- [ ] Star ratings and review counts on cards. Honest badges ("Best seller", "New")
- [ ] Simple sort and filter. Filters pruned to the ones people use
- [ ] Default sort deliberately set, e.g. manual by revenue per session, re-ranked monthly. Not "Newest" for first-time visitors
- [ ] Free-shipping notice and a line of social proof at the top
- [ ] SEO copy sits *below* the products, not above [user notes / OK]
- [ ] Breadcrumbs present (and backfilled for Google Shopping visitors)
- [ ] ⚖️ Quick add-to-cart on cards — good for cheap, multi-buy items; test it
- [ ] Unit price shown on multipacks

## 5. Product page (PDP)
**Above the fold (mobile):**
- [ ] 1:1 main image with visible thumbnails or arrows. A large image doesn't push the CTA down
- [ ] A short, clear title, with the benefit or outcome in it where natural
- [ ] Star rating + review count next to the title, specific ("4.8 from 3,200 reviews")
- [ ] Clear price. Saving shown as an amount ("Save £12"). Unit price for multipacks
- [ ] 3–5 benefit bullets with icons. Key info not hidden in dropdowns
- [ ] One primary CTA, contrasting colour, full width. Sticky on mobile. Competing CTAs (wishlist) removed or demoted
- [ ] Trust icons and payment logos anchored directly under the CTA
- [ ] In-stock message. Honest scarcity only ⚖️
- [ ] **One objection-answering review card directly under add-to-cart**
- [ ] If there's a fit question (size, compatibility, skin type), the CTA becomes a next step that resolves it ("Find your size")
- [ ] Estimated delivery shown as a specific date

**Below the fold (ATIDFA order):**
- [ ] Transformation: 3-step "how it works", ending on the benefit
- [ ] Benefits before features. Specs in a clean table
- [ ] Comparison table of checkable facts vs a "typical product" — only if you lead on measurable attributes
- [ ] Reviews: headline on each, quotes, bolded key phrases, full names, platform logo, photo reviews, a UGC gallery
- [ ] FAQ built from real customer objections (shipping, returns, sizing, care)
- [ ] A final CTA repeated at the bottom

**AOV:**
- [ ] Bundles and quantity breaks
- [ ] A relevant free bonus (a guide, an accessory), visible above the price
- [ ] Subscribe vs one-time as two clear choices, with "pause or cancel anytime"
- [ ] ⚖️ "Frequently bought together"
- [ ] ⚖️ Recently viewed — **returning visitors only**

## 6. Cart / cart drawer
- [ ] Clear item count
- [ ] Dynamic free-shipping or free-gift progress bar ("£8 away from free delivery")
- [ ] **Full cost shown: items + delivery + extras = total**, with "no extra fees at checkout" if true. Otherwise "Delivery from £X". Avoid the default "calculated at checkout" message
- [ ] Cross-sell or order bump with ratings and one-click add, ideally pageable
- [ ] Optional add-ons customers have asked for, e.g. gift wrap
- [ ] Ratings on cart items
- [ ] For high-ticket items: selling points, guarantee and a specific delivery date in the cart
- [ ] CTA reads "Continue to checkout". Payment logos and trust seals under it
- [ ] Express checkout options (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal)

## 7. Checkout (observe only; don't place orders)
- [ ] Guest checkout prominent. No forced account creation (18% of abandoners cite it)
- [ ] No surprise costs. Extra costs are the #1 abandonment reason (40% of non-browsing abandoners)
- [ ] Short form, ~7–8 fields, address autocomplete
- [ ] Clear security cues near card entry (19% cite distrust)
- [ ] Enough payment methods for the market (BNPL, wallets)
- [ ] Delivery speed and options clear (20% cite slow delivery)
- [ ] Returns policy visible (13% cite it)

## 8. Landing pages (paid traffic)
- [ ] Message match with the ad: same persona, problem, angle and imagery
- [ ] One goal, one CTA. Nav, social links and off-page exits removed
- [ ] ATIDFA structure top to bottom
- [ ] Testimonial above the fold
- [ ] Video in a modal, max 1–2
- [ ] Sticky mobile CTA
- [ ] Format fits the persona (PDP vs listicle vs advertorial vs quiz) — test it
- [ ] Shopify: consider rebuilding winning builder pages natively for speed

## 9. Thank-you / post-purchase
- [ ] A post-purchase survey: "What nearly stopped you buying?" and "How did you hear about us?" This feeds the objection reviews and the copy
- [ ] A post-purchase upsell or referral offer
- [ ] Clear delivery expectations and next steps

## 10. Technical and trust
- [ ] PageSpeed / Core Web Vitals reasonable, especially LCP on mobile. Treat speed as important but not a silver bullet
- [ ] No broken elements across browsers and devices
- [ ] Analytics and conversion tracking working (GA4 events: view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase) — otherwise nothing can be measured
- [ ] Product structured data complete: name, images, brand, SKU/GTIN, per-variant offers with price and availability, aggregateRating, BreadcrumbList, FAQ
- [ ] Specs as measurable facts (materials, dimensions, compatibility), not vague adjectives. AI shopping agents compare on these
- [ ] Shipping and returns policies in scannable, structured form on the PDP
- [ ] Accessibility basics: alt text, contrast, tap targets, keyboard focus
